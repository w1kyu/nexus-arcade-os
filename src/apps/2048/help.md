# 2048 Cyber

Slide matching tiles together on an NxN grid to merge them into higher powers of 2.

## How to Play

- **Swipe or Arrows**: Slide all tiles in one direction.
- **Merging**: When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one with double value!
- **Undo**: Made a misstep? Press Undo to revert up to 10 moves.
- **Board Sizes**: Choose from 3x3 (compact, difficult), 4x4 (standard), 5x5, 6x6, up to 8x8.
