# Blockfall

Blockfall is a falling-blocks puzzle game powered by the Nexus Arcade OS game engine.

## Rules & Mechanics

- **Tetrominoes:** 7 standard geometric pieces (I, J, L, O, S, T, Z) generated via a fair 7-bag randomizer.
- **Super Rotation System (SRS):** Full wall kick tables for both standard pieces and the I-bar, allowing rotations against walls and blocks.
- **Hold Slot:** Save a piece in reserve for when you need it most.
- **Ghost Projection:** Transparent outline shows exactly where your piece will land.
- **T-Spins:** Rotate a T-piece into tight 3-corner pockets for bonus points!

## Game Modes

- **Marathon:** Levels 1 through 15 with increasing gravity and difficulty.
- **Sprint:** Race against the clock to clear 40 lines as fast as possible.
- **Ultra:** 2-minute time trial to score as many points as you can.
- **Zen:** Infinite, relaxed play with steady gravity.
