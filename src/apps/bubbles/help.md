# Bubble Shooter

Aim your bottom launcher and fire colored bubbles to clear the descending ceiling grid.

## Mechanics

- **3-Match:** Match 3 or more connected bubbles of identical color to pop them.
- **Bank Shots:** Bounce shots off side walls to reach tricky clusters in tight angles!
- **Drop Cascades:** Any bubbles that lose all physical connections to the top ceiling row drop down immediately, yielding huge bonus points!
- **Ceiling Drops:** Every few shots that do not cause a pop, the ceiling shifts down. Don't let it reach the bottom border!
