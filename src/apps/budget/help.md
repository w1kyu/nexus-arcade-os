# Budget

Personal finance tracker with multiple accounts, monthly category budgets, income/expense reports, CSV bank import, and expense splitting.

## Features

- **Multiple Accounts**: Track checking accounts, cash, savings, and credit cards with running balances and total net worth.
- **Transactions**: Log income, expenses, and inter-account transfers with custom categories and notes.
- **Monthly Category Budgets**: Set spending limits per category with visual progress bars and over-budget warnings.
- **Reports**: Instant overview of monthly income vs. expenses, net balance, and spending breakdown.
- **Bank CSV Import & Export**: Flexible column-mapped CSV importer for bank statements and one-click CSV export.
- **Expense Splitter**: Fair "who owes whom" calculator that settles group debts with minimal transactions.
