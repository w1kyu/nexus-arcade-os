# Changelog — Calendar

## 1.0.0

- Day, Week, Month, Year, and Agenda views with mini-month navigator.
- Pointer drag-to-create, drag-to-move, and drag-edge-to-resize on the time grid.
- Multiple calendars with colors and visibility toggles.
- Overlays for Tasks with due dates, Timetable entries, and German public holidays (by state, including Hesse).
- RFC 5545 `.ics` import and export.
