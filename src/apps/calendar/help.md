# Calendar

Plan lectures, appointments, and deadlines across Day, Week, Month, Year, and Agenda views.

## Features

- **Views**: Switch between Day, Week, Month, Year, and Agenda views with a mini-month navigator and ISO week numbers.
- **Drag & Drop**: Drag on the Day or Week time grid to create an event, drag an event to move it, or drag its bottom edge to resize its duration.
- **Recurring Events**: Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly recurrence with support for editing only a single occurrence, this and following occurrences, or the entire series.
- **Overlays**: Toggle Tasks with due dates, University Timetable blocks, and German public holidays (computed locally per federal state, including Hesse).
- **`.ics` Import & Export**: Full RFC 5545 compatibility (`VEVENT`, `RRULE`, `EXDATE`, `VALARM`).
