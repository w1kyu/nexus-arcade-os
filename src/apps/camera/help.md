# Camera

Photo booth, video recording, and barcode scanner using your webcam.

## Features

- **Photo & Video Modes:** Capture high-resolution snapshots or record video clips.
- **Countdown Timer:** Set a 3s, 5s, or 10s delay with an on-screen timer.
- **Burst Mode:** Rapidly capture 3 continuous photos.
- **Live Visual Filters:** Real-time canvas filters including Grayscale, Sepia, Neon Edges, Pixelate, Mirror, Thermal Heatmap, and Cyberpunk.
- **QR / Barcode Scanner:** Scan QR codes and product barcodes directly through the live camera feed.
- **VFS Integration:** Photos are saved to `/Pictures` and videos to `/Videos`.
