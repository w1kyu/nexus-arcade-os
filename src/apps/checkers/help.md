# Checkers / Dame

Move diagonally over the dark squares, capture by jumping, and leave your opponent without a move.

## Rules

Captures are **mandatory** in both rule sets, and a multi-jump must be played to its end.

- **Standard (8×8):** men move and capture forwards only. Kings move one square in any direction. A man that reaches the last row is crowned and its move ends.
- **International (10×10):** men capture backwards too. Kings fly along whole diagonals. You must play the sequence that captures the **most** pieces. Jumped pieces are removed only after the move, and a man is crowned only if its move ends on the last row.

A side with no legal move loses. 40 moves by each side without a capture or a man move are a draw.

## Controls

- **Mouse / touch:** select a piece, then the square to move to. For multi-jumps, click each landing square in turn.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys move the cursor, Enter selects, Backspace deselects.
- **Gamepad:** d-pad or stick, A to select, B to deselect.
- **Escape:** pause menu.
