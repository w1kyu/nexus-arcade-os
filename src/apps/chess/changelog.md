# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

- Initial release of Nexus Chess.
- Full chess rules: castling, en passant, promotion, threefold repetition, 50-move rule, stalemate, insufficient material.
- AI search engine in Web Worker with 5 difficulty levels.
- Tactical puzzle challenges with hints and solutions.
- PGN import/export and move history review.
- Local 2-player mode and board flipping.
