# Changelog - Logic Circuit & Boolean Lab

## 1.0.0 (Phase 8)

- Real-time digital circuit simulator with interactive logic gates and wires.
- Circuit presets: Half Adder, Full Adder, SR Latch.
- Exhaustive circuit truth table generation.
- Boolean expression parser, evaluator, and equivalence checking.
- Karnaugh maps for 2 to 4 variables.
- Quine-McCluskey minimization with DNF and CNF outputs.
- PNG/SVG export and VFS persistence.
