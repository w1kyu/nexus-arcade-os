# Connect Four

Drop discs into the seven columns. The first player with four discs in a row — horizontal, vertical or diagonal — wins.

## Modes

- **vs AI:** you play cyan and move first. Easy, Medium and Hard search 2, 4 and 8 moves ahead.
- **2 Players:** take turns on the same device. Started from the Arcade lobby, each player can use their own gamepad.

## Controls

- **Mouse / touch:** click or tap a column.
- **Keyboard:** Left/Right to pick a column, Enter or Space to drop.
- **Gamepad:** d-pad or stick to pick, A to drop.
- **Escape:** pause menu.

Player 2's discs carry a ring, so the two sides can be told apart without color.
