# Void Drifter

A fast-paced vector arcade space combat game.

## Flight Dynamics

- **Thrust & Inertia:** Use Up or W to apply thrust. Your ship retains Newtonian momentum across the infinite toroidal screen space.
- **Hyperspace:** When surrounded, activate Hyperspace (Shift or H) to instantly jump across space. Beware of the 15% malfunction chance!

## Threats & Power-ups

- **Asteroids:** Large asteroids split into two medium asteroids, which each split into two small fragments.
- **UFOs:** Large flying saucers patrol the sector and fire randomly; small scout saucers actively track your flight vector.
- **Power-ups:** Destroying targets can drop Shield, Triple Shot, or Rapid Fire capsules.
