# Exam Planner

Exam countdowns, topic checklists, readiness scores, and automated study plan generation.

## Features

- **Countdown Cards**: Live day countdowns and times for all upcoming university exams.
- **Readiness Score**: Evaluates your exam readiness (0–100%) by combining checklist completion and remaining preparation time.
- **Topic Checklist**: Track granular exam topics and toggle completed items directly on the exam card.
- **Study Plan Generator**: Distribute remaining exam topics across days leading up to the exam into Nexus Tasks.
