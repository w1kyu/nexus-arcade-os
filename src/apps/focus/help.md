# Focus

Pomodoro and Flow timer with procedural Web Audio soundscapes, distraction blocking, and task time logging.

## Features

- **Pomodoro & Flow Modes**: Configurable work, short break, and long break durations, or count-up Flow mode, with a compact mini-window option.
- **Task Linking**: Link a focus session to any task from Tasks; completed focus minutes are logged directly onto the task.
- **Procedural Ambient Mixer**: Mix 10 synthesized Web Audio soundscapes (rain, thunderstorm, white/pink/brown noise, café murmur, fireplace crackle, ocean waves, forest, and 40 Hz binaural beats) and save custom presets.
- **Distraction Blocking & DND**: Automatically enables Do-Not-Disturb during work sessions and blocks games or selected apps with an override logger.
