# Neon Glide

Neon Glide is a one-button precision flight game with fluid inertia physics and dynamic synthwave obstacles.

## Controls

- **Flap / Thrust:** Spacebar, Up Arrow, W, Click / Tap on screen, or Gamepad Button A.
- **Pause:** Escape or P.

## Features

- Dynamic flap inertia and terminal velocity limits.
- Procedural neon obstacle gates that dynamically adapt to your score.
- Ghost of your best run that flies alongside your current attempt.
