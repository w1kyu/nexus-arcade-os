# Grade Tracker

ECTS-weighted GPA calculator, multi-scale support (German 1.0–5.0, US GPA, percentages), semester progress dashboard, and What-If scenario forecasting.

## Features

- **ECTS-Weighted GPA**: Accurately computes grade point average weighted by credit points for degree modules.
- **Multiple Scales**: Supports the German university scale (1.0–5.0 with 0.3/0.7 steps), US GPA (4.0–0.0), percentages (0–100%), and Swiss/Austrian grading systems.
- **What-If Calculator**: Calculates the exact average you need in remaining ECTS credits to graduate with your desired final grade.
- **Module Scenario Simulator**: Test hypothetical module grades to see their immediate impact on your degree average.
- **Partial Grades**: Combine exams, projects, and labs into weighted module grades.
