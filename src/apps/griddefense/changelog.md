# Changelog - Grid Defense

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

- Initial release for Nexus Arcade OS Phase 10 Tier 3.
- 11 handcrafted maps with single, multi-lane, and spiral paths.
- 6 tower types with 3 upgrade tiers and custom special effects.
- 6 creep varieties including Armored Tanks, Regenerators, and Colossus Bosses.
- Variable simulation speeds (1x, 2x, 4x) and pause support.
