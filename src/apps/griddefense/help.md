# Grid Defense

Protect your cyber core from waves of creeping malware and viral constructs across 10+ challenging network maps!

## How to Play

1. **Place Towers:** Select a turret from the bottom build bar and click on any buildable grid tile (roads and rocks are blocked).
2. **Tower Types:**
   - **Pulse Blaster (⚡ 100c):** High fire rate kinetic turret.
   - **Plasma Mortar (💥 175c):** Long range explosive shells with splash radius.
   - **Frost Emitter (❄️ 125c):** Freezes enemies and reduces creep movement speed.
   - **Tesla Coil (⚡ 200c):** Chain lightning arching between multiple targets.
   - **Railgun Sniper (🎯 250c):** Massive armor-piercing damage over long range.
   - **Void Disruptor (🌀 220c):** Energy drain field dealing sustained damage over time.
3. **Upgrades & Selling:** Click any existing tower to inspect its stats, toggle targeting priority (First, Last, Strongest, Weakest), upgrade up to Level 3, or sell for 70% refund.
4. **Speed Controls:** Fast forward the simulation at 1x, 2x, or 4x speed.
