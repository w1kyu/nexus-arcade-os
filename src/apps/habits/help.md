# Habits

Daily and weekly habit tracker with streak counters, 52-week activity heatmaps, and rest days.

## Features

- **Flexible Frequencies**: Daily, specific weekdays, or N times per week.
- **Multiple Goal Types**: Simple yes/no checkboxes, numerical counts (e.g., 8 glasses of water), or durations (e.g., 30 minutes reading).
- **Streak Counters**: Tracks current streak, best all-time streak, and overall completion rate.
- **Rest Days**: Take an intentional rest day without breaking your active streak.
- **Activity Heatmap**: Visual GitHub-style 52-week activity calendar for every habit.
- **Quick Check-Ins**: Mark habits done from the desktop widget, command palette, or terminal (`habit done <name>`).
