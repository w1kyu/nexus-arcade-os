# Hexword

Hexword is a 5-letter word puzzle available in English and German.

## Rules

- Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 attempts or fewer.
- Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.
- After submitting a guess, tiles flip to reveal clues:
  - 🟩 **Green:** Letter is in the correct position.
  - 🟨 **Yellow:** Letter is in the word, but in a different position.
  - ⬛ **Gray:** Letter does not appear in the word.

## Modes & Features

- **Daily Word:** A new word every day based on the calendar date.
- **Free Play:** Random words for unlimited practice.
- **Hard Mode:** Any revealed green or yellow clues must be used in subsequent guesses.
- **Emoji Grid:** Share your guessing journey to the clipboard with one click.
