# Grid Invaders

Grid Invaders is a vector arcade fixed-shooter.

## Controls

- Move Left/Right: Left / Right Arrow keys, A / D, or Gamepad D-Pad.
- Fire: Spacebar, J, or Gamepad Button A.
- Pause: Escape or P.

## Features

- Descending alien swarms that accelerate as their ranks thin.
- Destructible bunker shields that degrade as they absorb incoming fire.
- Special weapon capsules: Triple Shot, Rapid Fire, and Shield Boost.
- Boss Encounters every 5 waves against heavily armored motherships.
