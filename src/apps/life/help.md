# Game of Life Sandbox

Explore complex emergent behavior generated by simple mathematical rules.

## Controls & Features

- **Drawing:** Click or drag on the grid to activate or deactivate cells.
- **Rules:** Switch between classic Conway's Life, HighLife (with replicating patterns), Seeds, and Brian's Brain.
- **Pattern Stamping:** Select pre-assembled spaceships, oscillators, or the Gosper Glider Gun and stamp them onto the canvas with a single click!
- **Simulation:** Run continuously, single step, or adjust playback generation rate.
