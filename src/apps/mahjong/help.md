# Mahjong Solitaire

Match identical free tiles in pairs to clear the board.

## Rules

- A tile is **free** if no other tile rests on top of it, and it can slide out freely to either the left or the right.
- Tiles from the Dots, Bamboo, Characters, Winds, and Dragons suits match with their exact twin.
- **Seasons (春, 夏, 秋, 冬):** Any Season tile matches with any other Season tile!
- **Flowers (梅, 蘭, 竹, 菊):** Any Flower tile matches with any other Flower tile!
- **Verified Deals:** Every deal generated uses reverse pairing, guaranteeing a 100% solvable resolution without guessing.
