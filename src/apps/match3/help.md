# Match-3 Puzzle

Swap adjacent gems horizontally or vertically to create lines of 3 or more matching crystals.

## Rules & Special Gems

- **3-Match:** Removes the gems, causing those above to drop down with new gems filling from the top.
- **Cascade Combos:** Chain reactions increase your combo multiplier for massive bonus scores!
- **Level Goals:** Each level challenges you to collect a specific gem quota or target score before running out of moves.
