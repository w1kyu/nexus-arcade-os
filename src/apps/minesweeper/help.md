# Minesweeper

A modern take on the classic logic puzzle.

## Instructions

- **Goal:** Uncover all safe tiles without detonating any hidden mines.
- **Safe First Click:** Your very first click is guaranteed to be safe and will open up an initial area.
- **Numbers:** Each revealed number indicates how many mines are adjacent in the 8 surrounding cells.
- **Flags:** Right-click or switch to Flag mode to mark suspect cells with 🚩. Right-click again to mark with `?` or clear.
- **Chording:** Once you've flagged all adjacent mines around a number, click that number (or double-click) to reveal all remaining unflagged neighbors at once!
