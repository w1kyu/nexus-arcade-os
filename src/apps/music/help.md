# Nexus Music

Play and organize your local audio library offline with high fidelity.

## Features

- **Zero-Dependency Metadata Parsing:** Parses ID3v2, ID3v1, Vorbis Comments (OGG/FLAC), and MP4 metadata atoms (`.m4a`).
- **Views:** Browse by Songs, Albums, Artists, Playlists, or Folders.
- **10-Band Graphic Equalizer:** Custom frequency adjustments with built-in presets (Rock, Pop, Bass Boost, Treble Boost, Electronic).
- **Audio Visualizers:** 5 canvas visualizer modes: Spectrum Bars, Circular Radial, Oscilloscope Waveform, Bass Pulse, and 3D Starfield.
- **Synchronized Lyrics:** Supports `.lrc` files with real-time autoscrolling.
- **Media Session API:** Seamless integration with OS media keys and playback controls.
- **Mini Player Widget:** Keep music controls on your desktop.
