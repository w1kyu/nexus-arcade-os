# Nonogram (Picross)

Nonogram is a picture logic puzzle where cells in a grid must be colored or left blank according to numbers at the side of the grid.

## Rules

- The numbers beside each row and column indicate consecutive runs of filled cells.
- Multiple numbers have at least one blank space separating them (e.g. `2 3` means 2 filled cells, then one or more blanks, then 3 filled cells).
- Left-click (or tap) to fill a cell with neon ink.
- Right-click (or toggle the Cross tool `✕`) to mark a cell you know is empty.
- When an entire row or column matches its clues, the corresponding numbers automatically dim out.
- With "Error Penalty" enabled, misplacing a fill incurs an immediate time penalty.
