# Nexus Paint

Nexus Paint is a layered raster painting and digital art application for Nexus Arcade OS.

## Tools

- **Round Brush (`B`):** Smooth solid brush with pointer pressure sensitivity.
- **Airbrush:** Soft feathered brush for shading and airbrush gradients.
- **Pencil:** Single-pixel aliased precision drawing.
- **Spray Can:** Splatter and texture generator.
- **Eraser (`E`):** Transparent erase tool on the active layer.
- **Flood Fill Bucket (`G`):** Fast flood fill with intelligent color boundary detection.
- **Eyedropper (`I`):** Color picker that samples composite color under cursor.
- **Shapes:** Rectangle (`R`), Ellipse (`O`), and Line (`L`).
- **Text (`T`):** Type custom text directly onto the layer.
- **Symmetry Mode:** Real-time horizontal bilateral symmetry drawing.

## Layer Management

- Create, delete, duplicate and reorder layers in the right sidebar.
- Toggle layer visibility and adjust layer opacity from 0 to 100%.
- Blend modes: Normal, Multiply, Screen, Overlay.
- Merge layer down to combine artwork into a single layer.

## Image Adjustments

- **Brightness / Contrast:** Fine-tune tonal balance.
- **Hue / Saturation:** Color shifting and vibrance enhancement.
- **Grayscale & Invert:** Instant monochrome and negative effects.
- **Box Blur:** Smooth soften filter.

## Export & Saving

- Native `.npaint` JSON format preserves all layers and blend modes.
- Export clean PNG, JPEG, or WebP images.
