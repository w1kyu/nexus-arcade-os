# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial release of Pathfinding & Maze Lab for Phase 8.
- 6 pathfinding algorithms (BFS, DFS, Dijkstra, A*, Greedy, Bidirectional BFS).
- 5 maze generation algorithms.
- Grid editor with mud/weighting, start/goal dragging, and diagonal movement toggle.
- Pseudocode highlighting and VFS export.
