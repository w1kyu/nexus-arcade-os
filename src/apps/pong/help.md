# Neon Pong

A futuristic reimagining of the iconic arcade classic.

## Rules & Controls

- **First to 11:** Win 11 points to claim match victory.
- **Spin & Velocity:** Moving your paddle while making contact transfers vertical momentum and spin, dramatically changing the ball's trajectory.
- **Power-ups:** Pick up floating capsules in the arena:
  - **M:** Multi-ball (spawns extra ball in play)
  - **E:** Extended Paddle (+45% paddle length)
  - **S:** Speed Boost (accelerates ball)
  - **H:** Laser Shield (defends goal for 1 hit)
