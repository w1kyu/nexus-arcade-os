# Profile & Achievements

Track your progress across every Nexus Arcade OS app in one place.

## Features

- **Level & XP**: Earn XP by unlocking achievements across system workflows, productivity tools, and arcade games.
- **Achievements Gallery**: Filter all OS-wide, game, and app milestones by status (`All`, `Unlocked`, `Locked`). Secret achievements reveal their clues once discovered.
- **Activity Breakdown**: View tracked counters per app alongside your workspace file summary.
- **Nexus Wrapped**: Inspect your yearly milestone card and export a local summary report as text.
