# Neon Racer

Take the wheel in high-speed top-down cyber circuits against competitive AI drivers.

## Controls

- **Accelerate:** Up Arrow / W key
- **Brake / Reverse:** Down Arrow / S key
- **Steer:** Left & Right Arrows / A & D keys
- **Restart Race:** R key

## Features

- **Drift Mechanics:** Steer sharply into corners at top speeds to initiate cyber-drift.
- **Ghost Replay:** Your best lap recording appears as a transparent hologram racer to push your personal lap records even further!
- **5 Neon Circuits:** Race across the Neon Oval, Cyber Circuit, Hairpin Horizon, Matrix Speedway, and Quantum Vortex.
