# Snake Evolution

A futuristic reimagining of the arcade classic with interpolated physics, power-ups, and special map mechanics.

## Features

- **Smooth Motion**: Sub-pixel movement interpolation.
- **Input Buffering**: Queue rapid turns without missed inputs.
- **Power-Ups**: Speed boost, slow-motion, ghost form, shrink tail, magnet, double points, shield.
- **6 Maps**: Classic, Four Rooms, Cyber Labyrinth, Quantum Portals, Hazard Patrol, and Torus Drift.

## Controls

- **Arrows / WASD**: Steer snake.
- **Touch / Swipe**: On mobile or touchscreens, swipe in the direction you want to turn.
- **Gamepad**: D-pad or left analog stick.
- **P / Esc**: Pause menu.
