# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

- Initial release of Solitaire Collection.
- Added Klondike (Draw 1 and Draw 3), FreeCell, and Spider (1, 2, and 4 suits).
- Infinite undo history and auto-complete for won positions.
- Seeded random generator and deal replay.
- Comprehensive statistics and high scores integration.
