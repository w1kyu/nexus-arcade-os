# Solitaire Collection

Solitaire Collection features the three most popular patience games in clean, modern designs:

## Modes

- **Klondike (Draw 1 & Draw 3):** Build four suit foundations from Ace to King. Tableau stacks are built down in alternating colors. Drag stacks or click to move.
- **FreeCell:** All 52 cards are dealt face up across 8 cascades. Use the 4 free cells as holding spots to maneuver cards and build foundations.
- **Spider (1, 2, or 4 Suits):** Build complete descending runs from King to Ace in the tableau to clear entire suits.

## Controls & Features

- **Infinite Undo:** Step back as many moves as needed.
- **Auto-Complete:** When all cards are uncovered and the game is won, click Auto-Complete to cascade all cards into the foundations.
- **Solvable Seeds:** Every deal is seeded, so you can retry the exact same shuffle or try new seeds.
