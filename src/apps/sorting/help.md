# Sorting Visualizer

An interactive laboratory for learning and analyzing comparison and non-comparison sorting algorithms.

## Features

- **10 Algorithms**: Bubble, Selection, Insertion, Shell, Merge, Quick (Lomuto & Hoare with 5 pivot choices), Heap, Counting, Radix (LSD), and Bucket Sort.
- **Multiple Views**: Vertical bars, scatter dots, and 360° color wheel.
- **Step-by-Step Engine**: Play, pause, step forward/backward, and adjust execution speed.
- **Complexity Panel**: Time and space complexities, stability, and in-place properties.
- **Bilingual Pseudocode**: Active line highlighting in English and German.
- **Sound Synthesizer**: Audio pitch proportional to element value during comparisons.
- **Race Mode**: Race 2 to 4 algorithms side-by-side on identical input data.
- **VFS & Export**: Save and load visualizer states, export views as PNG or SVG.
