# Tasks

Manage your projects, sections, and daily tasks with natural-language quick add in English and German.

## Quick Add Examples

- `Prepare presentation tomorrow 14:00 #uni !high ~45m`
- `Präsentation vorbereiten morgen 14 Uhr #uni !hoch`
- `Weekly review friday at 5pm every week !p1 @University`

## Views

- **List:** Group by section, due date, project, or priority.
- **Kanban:** Drag & drop cards across custom columns with WIP limits.
- **Calendar:** 2-week overview of upcoming due dates.
- **Statistics:** Completed tasks chart, streak, average completion time, overdue rate, and tracked time per project.
