# Timetable

Weekly university course grid, biweekly (A/B) weeks, semester periods, and automated Calendar synchronization.

## Features

- **Weekly Course Grid**: Plan lectures, tutorials, labs, and seminars from 8:00 to 20:00 across weekdays.
- **Biweekly Turnus (A/B Weeks)**: Assign courses to run every week, only in week A, or only in week B.
- **Now & Next**: Instant view of your current active lecture and the countdown to your next upcoming class.
- **Calendar Overlay**: Timetable courses are automatically synced to the Calendar app via `~/.config/timetable-snapshot.json`.
- **Study Links**: Jump directly from any course to its dedicated notes folder in Nexus Notes, related tasks in Tasks, or deck in Flashcards.
- **Import & Export**: Export courses as RFC 5545 `.ics` or share the complete schedule as JSON.
