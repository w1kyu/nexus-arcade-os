# Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe

Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe is a strategic board game played on a nested 9x9 grid composed of 9 smaller 3x3 sub-boards.

## Rules of Play

1. **Targeting Rule:** The square you pick within any small 3x3 sub-board dictates which sub-board the opponent must play in next. For instance, playing in the top-right corner of a sub-board forces your opponent to play in the top-right sub-board!
2. **Sub-Board Victory:** Winning any 3x3 sub-board claims that tile on the giant macro board.
3. **Free Choice:** If sent to a sub-board that has already been won or is full, you have free choice to play anywhere on any open sub-board!
4. **Ultimate Win:** The first player to align 3 claimed sub-boards in a row, column, or diagonal wins the game!
