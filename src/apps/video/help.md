# Video Player

Watch local videos and movie clips offline with high performance.

## Features

- **Format Support:** Plays all browser-supported formats (`.mp4`, `.webm`, `.ogv`, `.mov`).
- **Frame Step Controls:** Use `<` and `>` buttons to inspect video frame-by-frame (1/30 second steps).
- **A-B Loop:** Set starting and ending timestamps to loop a specific section continuously.
- **Subtitles:** Supports loading external `.srt` and `.vtt` subtitle tracks.
- **Picture-in-Picture:** Keep watching video in a floating mini window while working in other apps.
- **Frame Capture:** Click the camera icon to save the current frame as a high-resolution PNG in `/Pictures`.
- **CSS Filters:** Apply live visual adjustments including High Contrast, Vibrant, Night Vision and Black & White.
