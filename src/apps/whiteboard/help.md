# Nexus Whiteboard

Nexus Whiteboard is an infinite canvas application for sketching, diagramming, brainstorming with sticky notes, and planning workflows.

## Tools and Shortcuts

- **Select & Move (`V`):** Click elements to select, drag to reposition, Shift+click for multi-selection.
- **Pen (`P`):** Freehand stroke drawing with pressure sensitivity.
- **Highlighter (`H`):** Semi-transparent marker for annotating and emphasizing.
- **Eraser (`E`):** Drag over elements to erase them.
- **Shapes:**
  - Rectangle (`R`)
  - Ellipse / Circle (`O`)
  - Line (`L`)
  - Arrow Connector (`A`)
- **Sticky Notes (`S`):** Color-coded sticky cards with multi-line notes. Double-click to edit text.
- **Text (`T`):** Click anywhere to place text. Double-click to edit.
- **Frames (`F`):** Group content into slides or presentation sections.

## Navigation

- **Pan:** Click and drag with mouse wheel, right mouse button, or hold Space + drag.
- **Zoom:** Use the mouse wheel with Ctrl/Cmd, pinch on trackpads, or use the bottom-right zoom widget.
- **Fit Content:** Click the Fit icon to center all board items automatically.

## Templates & Export

- **Templates:** Flowchart, Kanban Board, SWOT Analysis.
- **Export:** High-resolution PNG (`.png`), standalone SVG vector graphic (`.svg`), or native Nexus Board JSON (`.nboard`).
